Cheat Sheet: de & à

de — Origin & Source

Static — Origin (TO BE FROM)
de = of / belonging to
Je suis de Paris — I am from Paris
Dynamic — Source (TO COME FROM)
de = away / from
Je viens de Paris — I come from Paris

à — Location & Destination

Static — Location (TO BE AT)
à = at / in
Je suis à Paris — I am in Paris
Dynamic — Destination (TO GO TO)
à = to / toward
Je vais à Paris — I go to Paris
