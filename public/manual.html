0 In one breath
  • What this is: a homework helper for your French class.
  • What it is not: a course that replaces the class.
  • The course is a road with 50 stops. Each stop is one goal.
  • At each stop: guess → learn → practise → come back to what you missed.
  • Nothing is locked. Tap any stop any time.
  • Wrong answers never punish you. Effort still earns XP. Mistakes come back later to fix.

2 Finding your way

2.1 Front door /

  • Full-screen road (time of day matches real time).
  • Orange ENTER coin. Tap anywhere to enter.

2.2 Home = the map /home

  • 2D / 3D switch · zoom 30–200% · editable 🧑‍🎓 stop number (“where I am”).
  • 5 units × 10 stops. French titles: Bienvenue en classe · Qui suis-je ? · Ma vie, mes envies · En ville · À table !
  • Stop colours: 🔵 vocabulary 🩷 grammar 🟠 expressions 🟢 communication
  • 🚩 = where the class is. Road paved up to it, dotted beyond.
  • Done stop = pressed flat and paler, keeps its number. No tick.
  • 🏁 under the map = GramMarathon Finale.
  • Keyboard: type two digits (e.g. 31) to jump to that stop.

2.3 Goal page

  • One can-do sentence + up to 8 tiles (SpecuLearn, MneMemo, MémoiRecall, GramMarathon, VocabulaRain, LexicaLocker, ComposeIt, WorDrill).
  • Grey tile = nothing at this stop (not broken, not locked).
  • Swipe ↑↓ for other goals. Counter shows n / 50.

3 One goal, step by step
  1. Select the goal — map, or ☰ → GO TO → number → 🎯.
  2. Guess first: 💡 SpecuLearn. You are not meant to know it yet.
  3. Learn it: 📚 MneMemo (Idée → Formes → Exercice).
  4. Make it stick: 🃏 MémoiRecall.
  5. Practise: GramMarathon · ConjugaZone · WorDrill · ÉcouTexte · games.
  6. Come back: ❌ ErroReview.
  7. Stop is done when every learning activity it offers has been tried (games do not count).
Before the lessonDo SpecuLearn first, then come to class.
Before a testSame SpecuLearn to find holes, then practise + ErroReview. Finale helps for whole-course revision.
4 The activities

📝 Learn

💡 SpecuLearn

  • Multiple choice about the goal you picked. Guessing is the job.
  • 60 XP for finishing it, once per deck — never for the score, because it is a guess, not a test. No streak clock. No ErroReview. Wrong costs nothing.
  • Keys 1–4. Accents forgiven. Skip pretest jumps to the end.
  • Map, goals, Help, Favourites, and bug report work without sign-in. If SpecuLearn asks you to sign in on your build, do that — live auth can require it.

📚 MneMemo

  • Tabs: 🎯 Goal · 💡 Idée · 📐 Formes · 🏋️ Exercice.
  • Exercice levels (always 12 cards; harder ≠ shorter):
LevelWhat you do
★ FacileRecognise — pick / put words in order
★★ MoyenFill one gap
★★★ DifficileFill two gaps
🎁 BonusTranslate the whole sentence from English
  • Wrong cards come back once. Needs sign-in. A half-finished run can be resumed.

🃏 MémoiRecall

  • English front → French back. Views: One · All · List.
  • Étudier (flip · ✓ / ↺) or Me tester (type the French).
  • First try: 60 XP right / 20 XP wrong (× fire). Feeds the memory clock.

🔄 Drill

🏃 GramMarathon

  • Type the missing bit. No options. Accents can still count as right.
  • About 27 / 50 goals have gaps (else grey).
  • Finale (🏁): ~50 questions/day, at least one per goal, weighted to your weak spots.

🔤 ConjugaZone

  • Verb endings. REVEAL mode or TYPE IT mode.
  • On the swipe chain for goals that have verbs (~40 / 50).
  • Does not feed ErroReview (verb forms are separate from deck words).

❌ ErroReview

  • Words due today. Hear or read the French → pick the English.
  • 20 per batch. Badge = words due (not goals).
  • Right → 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 days. Wrong → due now.
  • Empty if you have never practised — normal for brand-new accounts.

🎮 Amuse (arcade — dessert)

  • Games do not finish a goal.

🌧️ VocabulaRain

  • Study table, then falling words into columns. Day → night → storm.
  • Ends when a column stacks to the top. Own score, and 60 XP the first time you finish it at a goal — then 60 again every time you beat that score.
  • Available on many stops (roughly 28–33 / 50). Expert countries pack costs 30💎.

🔢 Numbers — NumBus / NumBourse

  • Hear French numbers → type digits. Lives. Slow replay.
  • Counts for streak, and pays 60 XP the first time you finish — then 60 again on any run that beats your best.

🔐 LexicaLocker

  • Build French from syllable keys. ~28 / 50 goals. Misses can go to ErroReview.

💬 Speak

🎙️ WorDrill

  • Mic grades you: Parfait · Bien · same pronunciation · Presque · Not quite.
  • Fair to the mouth (sound-alikes can pass). Typing elsewhere is strict to the hand.

🎧 ÉcouTexte

  • Hear a mini-text · fill blanks · speed / voice controls.
  • “Show the sentence” can send words to ErroReview.

🔊 VoixLà

  • Type French → hear it → check corrections → optional MP3.
  • Needs sign-in (protects the cloud voice bill).
  • Does not spend your gems or XP.

🛠️ Write

🤖 ChaTutor

  • Ask anything in French or English. Needs sign-in + live server.
  • Not a drill. Does not write the memory clock.
  • Tip: ask why an answer is right — then close it and answer yourself.

🧩 ComposeIt

  • Role-play scenes with phrase chips. Finish → 120 XP.
  • Available on a set of scene goals (includes communication ateliers 10, 20, 30, 40, 49, 50).

👤 Track

  • Profile: streak, XP, gems, badges, “do this next”, heat strip of 50 stops.
  • Leaderboard: default This week (resets Monday) · All term = lifetime XP. Gems do not rank you.
  • Settings: icon labels · map wheel · bottom bar · gem shop.
5 How activities connect
  • One deck of words per goal — many doors into the same list.
  • SpecuLearn is outside the loop — records nothing on purpose.
  • Feeds ErroReview: MneMemo exercise, MémoiRecall (test), GramMarathon, WorDrill, ÉcouTexte, LexicaLocker, some game misses.
  • Does not: SpecuLearn, VoixLà, ChaTutor, ConjugaZone, NumBus, NumBourse.
  • Finish screens point to Next › — first useful undone step at this stop.
6 XP · streak · gems · done

Nothing you can open here pays nothing. Every activity is worth XP at least once, and the ones that are not marked per answer pay for the run instead.

You doYou get
Right, first try60 × fire
Wrong, first try20 × fire
Same item again in that run0 more XP
Wrong then right in one run20 total (not 20+60)
Goal newly done300 + up to 300 mastery
ComposeIt scene finished120
SpecuLearn · VocabulaRain · NumBus · NumBourse — first finish60
… and again every run that beats your own best60
A run that does not beat your best0 — and nothing is taken away

🏆 Beating your own best

  • Play a game all afternoon if you like: every genuine improvement pays again. A worse run simply pays nothing.
  • Your best is kept per game, per goal — a record on the Unit 0 deck is not a record on Unit 3.
  • SpecuLearn is the exception: it pays for finishing, once per deck, never for the score. It is the guess before the lesson — if the score paid, the clever move would be to do the lesson first, and the activity would stop measuring anything.

🔥 Streak / fire

  • Any graded answer that day (right or wrong). Numbers games count.
  • Day rolls at 4 am local time.
  • Multipliers: 3d ×1.5 · 7d ×2 · 14d ×2.5 · 30d ×3.
  • Wrong answers never break the streak. Missing a calendar day can.
  • ❄️ Streak-Freezer: 25💎, hold max 2, buy before you miss.

🎚️ Levels

  • 1 @ 2 000 · 2 @ 6 000 · 3 @ 14 000 · 4 @ 30 000 XP.

💎 Gems — what you start with

  • You begin with 20💎, free, once. That is the same as one level-up, and the same as the cheapest colour in the shop — so you can read your purse against a price you can see. It is roughly three exercises’ worth of lucky finds: enough that your first session never runs into a price you cannot pay.
  • After that they are earned, and they come in faster than the purse: a twenty-item exercise finds about 6💎.

💎 Gems — where the rest come from

  • Lucky finds — about 1 answer in 8 pays 2💎 (rarely 10💎), guaranteed by the 12th find-less answer, capped at 40💎 a day. A find can only ever give: there is no bad outcome and nothing is ever taken away.
  • Badges — 5–50💎 each, paid the first time you earn one (goals: 5·10·20·50 · streaks: 5·10·30 · words mastered: 15·30).
  • Level-up — +20💎 each, four times over French 1.
  • Over all fifty goals a thorough learner earns roughly 1 450💎. The entire shop costs 205💎, so gems are never the thing standing between you and the course.

💎 Gems — what they buy

  • Colours (20–50💎) · Streak-Freezer (25💎) · Expert countries pack (30💎).
  • Never a lesson, a drill or a revision. Nothing you need in order to pass has a price on it.

✓ When a stop is done

  • Tried every learning door it offers (right or wrong).
  • Games are extras — not required.
  • Looks pressed flat and paler. No green tick. Stays done forever.
7 Sign-in · saving · devices
  • Sign in with Google for lessons, drills, games, ErroReview, VoixLà, ChaTutor, leaderboard, history.
  • Without sign-in you can still browse the map, goal pages, Help, Favourites, and report bugs.
  • Signed in → cloud save across devices.
  • Signing out clears learning data on that device (cloud copy kept).
  • Install as an app (PWA) after a few visits.
8 Good to know
  • Chrome/UI is English on purpose; French is the learning content.
  • Spoken = gentle (fair to the mouth). Typed = strict (letters/accents).
  • Teacher dashboard exists but is not linked for students.

You will NOT find (on purpose)

  • No hearts / lockouts in drills.
  • No locked lessons. Gems buy decoration only.
  • No “mark as done” button.
  • No scoring economy inside SpecuLearn.

Study plan — whole course

Same loop for every goal. The course is 50 goals in 5 units. Work along the road; do not skip the loop.

The loop (use on every goal)

  1. Before class for that goal: 💡 SpecuLearn.
  2. Learn: 📚 MneMemo → 🃏 MémoiRecall.
  3. After class: one Drill and/or Speak item that is lit up.
  4. Optional: one Amuse game · ComposeIt if the tile is lit.
  5. Ongoing: ❌ ErroReview whenever the badge shows due words.
  6. Before tests: SpecuLearn again · ErroReview empty · 🏁 Finale for whole-course revision.

Week 1 — get the habit (Unité 0, start)

  • Day 1 — Map · Goal 1 · SpecuLearn.
  • Day 2 — MneMemo · MémoiRecall.
  • Day 3 — One drill + one game.
  • Day 4 — WorDrill · try VoixLà.
  • Day 5 — First ErroReview.
  • Day 6 — ComposeIt if available (e.g. greetings / meeting).
  • Day 7 — Profile · continue Goals 2–3 with the same loop.

Unité 0 · Goals 1–10 · « Bienvenue en classe »

  • Do Goals 1 → 9 with the loop above (about 1–2 goals per study day).
  • Goal 10 is the first communication atelier — use ComposeIt / role-play when lit.
  • Clear ErroReview before you leave the unit.

Unité 1 · Goals 11–20 · « Qui suis-je ? »

  • Same loop. Expect more grammar tiles (être, avoir, nationalities…).
  • Use 🔤 ConjugaZone when verbs appear.
  • Goal 20 = short writing (country). Prepare, then ComposeIt / write practice if offered.

Unité 2 · Goals 21–30 · « Ma vie, mes envies »

  • Same loop. Likes, places, time, negation, invitations.
  • Goal 30 = short email / wishes — writing focus.
  • Keep ErroReview short and frequent (many forms pile up here).

Unité 3 · Goals 31–40 · « En ville »

  • Same loop. Weather, town, questions, directions, transport.
  • Goal 40 = itinerary — produce connected steps.
  • WorDrill + ÉcouTexte help a lot for directions language.

Unité 4 · Goals 41–50 · « À table ! »

  • Same loop. Food, shops, plans, advice, restaurant.
  • Goals 49–50 = review + restaurant role-play — end-of-course production.
  • Before the final test: ErroReview empty · 🏁 Finale · SpecuLearn on weak goals.

Missed days? Nothing breaks. Map keeps your place. ErroReview keeps your misses. A freezer (if bought) keeps your fire. Pick up the same loop on the next glowing stop.
