# Specific Instructional Objectives — the 50 stops

One Specific Instructional Objective per stop, formulated entirely from the
content of that stop (`src/content/sios/sios.json`, generated from
`docs/handoff/LAF1201_SIOs_Flashcards_v9.csv`). Each objective states the
observable behaviour the learner will be able to perform on completing the
stop, the conditions, and the criterion of acceptable performance drawn from
that stop's competence spec.

## Unité 0 — Bonjour, bienvenue, enchanté !

**SIO-001 · Introductions** — On completing this stop, the learner will be
able to introduce themselves and a third person (*Je m'appelle…*, *Il/Elle
s'appelle…*), ask someone's name with both *tu* (*Comment tu t'appelles ?*)
and *vous* (*Comment vous vous appelez ?*), and conjugate *s'appeler* across
all 8 subject forms drilled in the deck (*je* → *elles*), with at least 5/6
correct. (*M./Mme* as forms of address is assessed under SIO-006, where its
content lives.)

**SIO-002 · Tu / Vous** — Given the 12 interlocutors in the deck (*un
copain*, *le professeur*, *une vendeuse*…), the learner will classify each
under the appropriate pronoun — *tu*, polite singular *vous*, or plural
*vous* — in at least 10 of the 12. (The reason for each choice is revealed as
feedback after the attempt; the learner selects, and is not asked to produce
a justification.)

**SIO-003 · Alphabet** — The learner will name all 26 letters of the French
alphabet, sort them into the 7 vowel-sound groups (ah · ay · air · i · ü ·
oh · uh), and spell a given word aloud with at least 90% of the letters
correct, asking or answering *Comment ça s'écrit ?*

**SIO-004 · Days + moments** — The learner will list the 7 days of the week
in order and the 3 moments of the day (*matin, après-midi, soir*), classify
any given day as weekday or weekend, and ask and answer *C'est quand ?*

**SIO-005 · Colours** — The learner will name the 12 colours in French and
give the matching mnemonic object (*le feu rouge*, *le flamant rose*…) for at
least 10 of the 12.

**SIO-006 · Core nouns — people, things, places** — Given the 18 core nouns
(*un prénom* … *un micro*), the learner will label at least 15 with the
correct article *un/une*, answer *C'est où ? / C'est qui ? / C'est quoi ?*
appropriately for a given place, person, or object, and choose *Monsieur
(M.)* or *Madame (Mme)* as the form of address for a given person.

**SIO-007 · Numbers 0–20** — The learner will read aloud and say any number
from 0 to 20, explain 17–19 as composites (*dix-sept* = 10 + 7), say 20, and
answer *Il y a combien d'étudiants ?*

**SIO-008 · Classroom instructions** — On hearing any of the 9 classroom
instructions (*Écoutez !, Regardez !, Répétez !, Lisez !, Écrivez !,
Parlez !, Notez !, Comptez !, Associez !*), the learner will perform the
correct action for at least 8 of the 9, and will ask *On fait quoi ?* when
unsure what to do.

**SIO-009 · Greetings** — Given a time of day and a relationship (casual or
formal), the learner will produce a register-appropriate greeting or
leave-taking in at least 8 of 10 prompts, and sort greeting phrases into
meeting vs parting and universal vs familiar.

**SIO-010 · First meeting role-play** — The learner will carry out a complete
first-time meeting in French, performing every step of the chain without
omission: greet, ask a name (with *tu* or *vous*), give their own name, ask
*Et toi ?*, ask and answer *Comment ça s'écrit ?*, say *Enchanté(e)*, and
take leave with *Au revoir !*

## Unité 1 — C'est qui, le monsieur ?

**SIO-011 · Stressed pronouns** — The learner will identify each of the 8
stressed pronouns (*moi, toi, lui, elle, nous, vous, eux, elles*) by its
position — after a preposition, after *c'est*, or standing alone — and match
at least 7 of the 8 to their subject pronouns.

**SIO-012 · Professions** — The learner will name 10 professions in both
masculine and feminine forms and present a person's job with *il est / elle
est ___* or *C'est un/une ___* correctly for at least 8 of the 10.

**SIO-013 · School subjects** — The learner will name academic subjects with
the correct article, ask *Qu'est-ce que tu étudies ?*, and answer with *Je
suis étudiant(e) en ___*.

**SIO-014 · Subject pronouns + ÊTRE** — The learner will conjugate *être* in
all 8 persons with at least 7/8 correct and apply gender and number agreement
to *étudiant / étudiante / étudiants / étudiantes*.

**SIO-015 · Countries** — The learner will name 25 countries with the correct
article (*le, la, l', les*, or no article) for at least 20 of the 25, and ask
*C'est quel pays ?*

**SIO-016 · Nationalities** — For the 25 countries, the learner will produce
the nationality adjective in all 4 forms (m.sg, f.sg, m.pl, f.pl) and link
country to nationality for at least 20 of the 25.

**SIO-017 · Languages** — The learner will match each of the 25 countries to
the language(s) spoken there, using *on parle ___*, for at least 20 of the 25.

**SIO-018 · Numbers 20–69** — The learner will read aloud and say any number
from 20 to 69, identify the 6 tens, and build the composites 21–69 — with *et
un* where required — at 90% accuracy or better.

**SIO-019 · Avoir — age & states** — The learner will conjugate *avoir* with
at least 7/8 correct, state an age with *J'ai ___ ans*, and sort the deck's
11 states into their two families with at least 5/6 correct: *avoir* + noun
(*faim, soif, chaud, froid, envie, besoin*) vs *être* + adjective (*fatigué,
content, malade, triste, calme*), applying singular/plural agreement to the
*être* adjectives. (Negative forms are not drilled in this stop's content —
negation is built at SIO-028.)

**SIO-020 · Mini-text: present a country** — With time to prepare, the
learner will write 3–4 comprehensible sentences presenting a francophone
country, including all 4 required elements: name, location, language, and one
cultural fact.

## Unité 2 — On fait quoi ce week-end ?

**SIO-021 · Everyday objects** — The learner will present at least 6 of the
14 everyday objects (*un sac* … *une clé*) using *c'est + un/une + N* and *ce
sont + des + N*, and ask *C'est quoi ?*

**SIO-022 · Possessives** — Given at least 8 prompts, the learner will select
the possessive adjective (*mon/ma/mes, ton/ta/tes, son/sa/ses,
notre/votre/leur/leurs*) that agrees with the gender and number of the noun
possessed — not the owner — with at least 80% accuracy.

**SIO-023 · Leisure activities — j'aime, j'adore** — The learner will
conjugate *aimer* in all persons and express likes with both *aimer* +
definite article + noun (*J'aime le cinéma*) and *aimer* + infinitive
(*J'aime lire*), producing at least 2 examples of each pattern.

**SIO-024 · faire + article contraction** — The learner will say what sports
and activities they do using *faire* with the correct article contraction
(*du / de la / de l' / des*) for at least 6 activities, with at least 80%
accuracy.

**SIO-025 · pourquoi ? parce que** — The learner will ask *Pourquoi ?* and
give a simple reason for a preference with *parce que c'est* + adjective or
*parce que j'aime* + noun, for at least 3 prompts.

**SIO-026 · aller + article contraction** — The learner will say where they
go using *aller* with the correct article contraction (*au / à la / à l' /
aux*) for at least 6 destinations, with at least 80% accuracy.

**SIO-027 · Time — when I do it** — The learner will say when they do their
regular activities using *le* + day, *le week-end*, *tous les
jours/soirs/matins*, and *à* + clock time (*à 8 heures, à midi, à minuit*)
for at least 5 prompts, and sort the expressions into day, frequency, and
clock time.

**SIO-028 · Négation — pas de ou pas le ?** — The learner will negate with
*ne … pas* using the correct article family: *pas de/d'* after *faire*, *il y
a*, and quantities (*Je ne fais pas de tennis*), while keeping *le/la/les*
after *aimer*, *adorer*, and *détester* (*Je n'aime pas le tennis*).

**SIO-029 · vouloir — invite, accept, refuse, reschedule** — In a short
dialogue, the learner will use *vouloir/pouvoir* to perform all 4 functions:
invite (*Tu veux venir ?*), accept (*Je veux bien !*), refuse (*Je ne peux
pas*), and reschedule (*On peut se voir samedi ?*).

**SIO-030 · Well wishes + connectors** — With time to prepare, the learner
will produce the well-wish formulas (*Bon anniversaire !, Bonne chance !, Bon
voyage !, Bonne année !, Bonne fête !*) and write a 4–6 sentence holiday
email using at least 3 of the connectors *d'abord, ensuite, aussi, mais, et,
enfin* correctly.

## Unité 3 — On va où cet été ?

**SIO-031 · Weather** — The learner will describe the weather using at least
6 expressions (*Il fait beau/chaud/froid/mauvais, il pleut, il neige, il y a
du vent/du soleil/des nuages*) with at least 80% accuracy, and ask *Quel
temps fait-il ?*

**SIO-032 · en / au / aux / à — countries** — The learner will say which
country they are in, going to, or coming from, using *en/au/aux/à* with
*être*, *aller*, and *venir* in at least 6 sentences, with at least 80%
accuracy.

**SIO-033 · Places in town** — The learner will say where they are, where
they are going, and where they are coming from around town, using
*être/aller/venir* with the correct contraction (*au/à la/à l'/aux, du/de
la/de l'*) in at least 6 sentences, with at least 80% accuracy.

**SIO-034 · Locating places** — The learner will say where a place is in
relation to another using the spatial prepositions *à côté de, en face de,
près de, loin de* with the correct article contraction, for at least 4
prompts.

**SIO-035 · Yes/no and open-ended questions** — The learner will form a
yes/no question with *Est-ce que… ?* and with intonation, and an open
question with each of the 7 question words (*Où, Quand, Comment, Pourquoi,
Qu'est-ce que, Qui, Combien de*), with at least 80% accuracy.

**SIO-036 · Directions + ordinal numbers** — The learner will ask for and
give directions without using the imperative, producing the *vous* + present
pattern the deck drills (*Vous allez tout droit. / Vous tournez à gauche. /
Vous prenez la première rue à droite. / C'est au bout de la rue.*) and
understanding the same itinerary steps in the *il faut* + infinitive and
*on* + present variants met in the reading texts (*il faut prendre la
deuxième rue à gauche / on va tout droit jusqu'au carrefour*); the learner
will use the ordinals *première* and *deuxième* — the only ordinals this
stop's content shows — in street directions, correctly for at least 4
prompts at 80% or better.

**SIO-037 · pouvoir — what one can do** — The learner will conjugate
*pouvoir* in its key persons and produce at least 3 statements about what is
possible or allowed in a place, or requests for permission, using *on peut /
je peux / vous pouvez* + infinitive.

**SIO-038 · Getting around** — The learner will ask how someone gets
somewhere (*Comment tu vas à l'école ?*), answer with *prendre* + mode of
transport, and use the pronoun *y* (*J'y vais en bus. / On y va ?*) in at
least 3 sentences.

**SIO-039 · Wants and needs** — The learner will express wants and needs
politely using all 5 frames the deck teaches — *je voudrais, j'aimerais,
j'ai envie de, j'ai besoin de, je veux* — each followed by a noun or an
infinitive, as drilled in the deck's two examples per frame.

**SIO-040 · Itinerary steps with connectors** — The learner will explain step
by step how to get somewhere, describing at least 4 ordered steps linked with
*d'abord, puis, ensuite, après, enfin/finalement*, giving each step in the
present tense with an explicit subject, as the model itinerary does (*D'abord,
tu prends la première rue à droite. Ensuite, tu vas tout droit.* — no
imperative), combined with transport and direction vocabulary.

## Unité 4 — Qu'est-ce qu'on mange ce soir ?

**SIO-041 · Aliments + meals** — The learner will name the 4 meals
(*petit-déjeuner, déjeuner, goûter, dîner*) and at least 2 foods or drinks
for each, and say what they eat and drink at each meal, with at least 80%
accuracy.

**SIO-042 · Partitives + manger/boire** — The learner will distinguish liking
(*J'aime le pain* — definite) from consuming (*Je mange du pain* — partitive)
in at least 8 prompts with at least 80% accuracy, rewrite at least 5
negatives with *de/d'* (*Je ne mange pas de viande*), use quantity
expressions with *de/d'* (*beaucoup de pain, assez de sel, trop de sucre, un
peu de lait, un kilo de pommes*) as the deck's quantities category drills,
and conjugate *manger* and *boire* in all persons with at least 7/8 correct
for each.

**SIO-043 · Frequency adverbs** — The learner will describe habits using the
5 frequency adverbs (*toujours, souvent, parfois, rarement, ne… jamais*),
placing the adverb correctly after the verb in at least 5 sentences, and
order the adverbs on a frequency scale.

**SIO-044 · Commerces — shopping and the market exchange** — The learner will
match at least 5 food shops (*boulangerie, épicerie, boucherie, marché,
supermarché*) to what they sell, say where they shop, ask *Combien ça
coûte ?* and *Je voudrais ___, s'il vous plaît*, and handle a market exchange
on either side of the stall: assign each line of the dialogue to *le
marchand* or *le client*, put the exchange in order, and produce *Je
voudrais…*, *Ça fait combien ?*, and *Et avec ceci ?*

**SIO-045A · Numbers 70–99** — The learner will count from 70 to 99 following
the *soixante-dix* and *quatre-vingts* patterns, and understand and produce
these numbers in market prices (*Ça fait quatre-vingt-cinq euros.*).

**SIO-046 · Demonstratives** — The learner will point out specific items when
shopping or ordering by selecting *ce/cet/cette/ces* correctly by gender and
number (*ce pain, cet ananas, cette tarte, ces légumes*) for at least 8
nouns, with at least 80% accuracy.

**SIO-047 · Making plans — aller + infinitif** — The learner will say what
they are going to do — today, this weekend, after class — producing at least
5 futur-proche sentences with correctly conjugated *aller* + infinitive
(*Je vais manger. / Nous allons visiter le marché.*).

**SIO-048 · Giving advice — devoir / falloir / pouvoir** — The learner will
give at least 5 pieces of simple advice about food, health, and daily life
using *tu dois* + infinitive (personal obligation), *il faut* + infinitive
(general necessity), and *tu peux* + infinitive (suggestion), with correct
verb forms (*Tu dois dormir plus. / Il faut manger des légumes. / Tu peux
prendre le bus.*).

**SIO-049 · Reviewing a restaurant** — The learner will give a simple
restaurant review stating at least 1 positive point (*C'est délicieux !*), at
least 1 negative point (*Le service est lent.*), and a clear recommendation
(*Je recommande / Je ne recommande pas*).

**SIO-050 · Role-play: restaurant scene** — The learner will get through a
complete restaurant visit in role-play, performing all 6 steps: greet the
waiter, read the menu, order (*entrée/plat/boisson/dessert*), interact during
the meal, ask for the bill, and pay and say goodbye.
