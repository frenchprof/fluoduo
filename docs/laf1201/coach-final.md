# Guided writing coach v2 (Dan, 30 Oct). This replaces coach-scenarios.md.

Copy every French string VERBATIM. Do not add, reword or invent French.

## The bug this fixes
The app printed « Il faut visiter au Jardin botanique ». It glued the chosen verb onto a
FIXED « au Jardin botanique ». From now on, NOTHING after the verb is pre-glued: the student
picks the linking word too, and the preview shows only what the student has actually picked.

## Step 1 · Plan (simpler, as Dan drew it)
```
STEP 1 · PLAN
Your friend Lucas will come to Singapore for a month.
(Ton ami Lucas va venir à Singapour pour un mois.)

Pick 5 things to advise him on          ← big and bold (sentence case, NOT capitals)

[ 8 small topic tiles ]                  ✓ 5 chosen      [ Next → ]
```
- The English line is plain text. The French line sits under it, in brackets, at the SAME size or smaller.
- The instruction is the biggest text on the screen: bold, in sentence case (Dan: not capitalised).
- Each topic tile shows an emoji + the NOUN ONLY, with NO article (e.g. « 🚇 métro »). The article is what the student must choose in Step 2.

## Step 2 · Build: FOUR choices now, in this order

**Per topic, ONLY the listed builds are accepted; there can be 1 or 2 (verb + link). Show the 4 verb tiles = the accepted verb(s) + the listed wrong verbs, shuffled. Show the 4 link tiles listed. Show ONLY that topic's reasons (2–3, all correct), so every finished sentence is sensible advice.**

Check order: (1) HOW — a NEG topic needs « Il ne faut pas », any other topic needs « Il faut » or « Tu peux »; (2) verb is one of the accepted verbs; (3) link matches THAT verb's accepted link; (4) a reason is picked. Feedback for (3) is the topic's link rule. The preview prints only the student's own picks, with « ___ » for anything not yet picked; the app never adds an article or preposition by itself.
```
STEP 2 · BUILD · ADVICE 1 OF 5          🚇 métro
1. HOW?        [Il faut] [Tu peux]                       (B adds [Il ne faut pas])
2. WHICH VERB? [prendre] [boire] [visiter] [aller]
3. LINK WORD   [le] [la] [du] [au]       → ___ métro
4. WHY?        [parce que c'est rapide] …
Your sentence: Il faut prendre ___ métro …           ← only what has been picked so far
[ Check ]   [ Skip — I'll write it myself ]
```
- Live preview: before a link word is picked, show a blank « ___ », never a guessed article.
- **Check, in order:**
  1. **Verb wrong:** "In French you *take* the metro: prendre." The link word is not judged yet.
  2. **Verb right, link word wrong:** a one-line rule in English. The table below gives the rule for each topic.
  3. **For NEG topics (scenario B), the HOW choice must be « Il ne faut pas ».**
  4. The HOW and WHY choices are otherwise free.
- Retry until right, the same as Practice. Log each attempt: topic, the 4 choices, which part was wrong, ms.
- Feedback shows correct French only. Never print a wrong combination as a model.

## Smaller tiles
Tiles and chips must be about 25–30% smaller than now: smaller padding and text one step down. All 4 rows plus Check must fit a 390×844 screen without scrolling. Topic tiles: 2 columns, compact. Do not shrink the French below the English anywhere on the card.
