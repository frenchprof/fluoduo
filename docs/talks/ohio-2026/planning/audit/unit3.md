**The verdicts:**
- **OVER**: the ceilings file names the item as out of scope, or places it in a later unit.
- **NOT LISTED**: the item is missing from the ceilings row's own list of what the book covers for that piece (box + Mémo + activities + answer keys).
- **TEST-ONLY**: the item appears only in the guide's test or model answers. Ceilings §D says this is **Dan's call**, so it is counted under "unclear".
- **UNCLEAR**: the ceilings file does not settle it.

Distractors are flagged only when they use later-unit vocabulary or grammar, or when their explanation teaches it.

## 3. Whole-goal issues

1. **SIO-037 « pouvoir — what one can do »: the goal itself is over the ceiling.** The book gives pouvoir no box before Unit 6 (table of contents, Bk p.11; ceilings Pouvoir row, §A, §B). Only the chunks *je peux / tu peux / on peut / vous pouvez* appear, incidentally. Yet the deck, the pretest, the pouvoir lesson (six-person table), the modaux lesson (which adds the whole of **devoir** and **il faut**) and ConjugaZone all teach a paradigm. Proposal: MOVE the goal → unit 6, or reduce it to the four seen chunks as receptive phrases, unlink `modaux` from it, and drop the « conjugate pouvoir (key persons) » competence. **ASK DAN.**
2. **SIO-034 « 7 question words »: half the goal is later units.** The U3 book point is *est-ce que / qu'est-ce que* only (Bk p.48). *Comment / où / pourquoi* questions are "Les questions (3)" in **Unit 6**. *Combien* belongs to the U4 shop dialogue. The competence (« an open question with each of the 7 question words ») and `question-words.json` both over-teach. Proposal: SIMPLIFY the goal → "Est-ce que…? (oui/non) and Qu'est-ce que…? (open)", keeping *où / qui / quoi / comment* only as the set phrases already met. Also unresolved: *si* and *n'est-ce pas* (questions-oui-non lesson). The ceilings do not mention either. **ASK DAN.**
3. **SIO-040 connectors (d'abord, puis, ensuite, après, enfin, finalement): UNCLEAR.** The ceilings file's Unit 3 rows never mention sequencing connectors. The book's direction model is *Vous prenez la première rue à droite, vous continuez tout droit, vous tournez à droite* (Bk p.50), with no connectors. The whole goal, its atelier, and both ComposeIt banks rest on them. **ASK DAN** whether the U3 atelier page (Bk p.55; not in the ceilings rows) covers them.
4. **SIO-036 builds in il faut (U4) and au bout de (out).** The goal's own description asks learners to understand *il faut + infinitive*, and the pretest and finale drill it. *Il faut* is the Unit 4 conseil box (Bk p.65). Proposal: remove *il faut* from the SIO-036 competence; SIMPLIFY → *vous + present* only.
5. **SIO-033 / SIO-035 / SIO-036 rest on test-only places.** *gare, banque, poste, hôpital, école, université* appear in the book only in the guide's unit test (ceilings Lieux row, §D). They are the default places in nearly every unit-3 surface, including the atelier model and the SIO-036 `fr` line « Pour aller à la gare ? ». *pharmacie* and *mairie* are named out of scope and should go regardless. **ASK DAN** on the test-only six. The cut affects every unit-3 surface at once.
6. **Six-person aller (nous allons / ils vont) across SIO-032/033/038.** The book boxes four persons (U2); the guide's test asks for the other two (§B, §D). Every unit-3 deck, lesson and textgen uses them. **ASK DAN.**
