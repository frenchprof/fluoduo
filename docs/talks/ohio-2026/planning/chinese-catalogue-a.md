# NPCR (新实用汉语课本, 2nd ed.) Textbook 1, Lessons 1–7: ceiling catalogue

Source: Dan's scan, `practicalchineseT1.pdf` (270 pp., no text layer). Every claim below was read off the page images.

**Page offset: PDF page = printed page + 24** (printed p.3, the start of Lesson 1, is PDF p.27). The front matter uses roman numerals (TOC 1–8 = PDF 7–14, Preface I–IX = PDF 15–23).

Lesson spans (printed pages): L1 3–13 · L2 14–23 · L3 24–35 · L4 36–47 · L5 48–61 · L6 62–76 · L7 77–94 · L8 starts 95.

## Conventions the book itself states

- **`*` on a new word** = "Words marked by an asterisk have appeared in previous lessons" (p.15 footnote).
- **Purple new words** = "Students are required to master the characters of the purple new words in this lesson" (p.5 footnote). The scan's pinks and purples are faint, so this catalogue does not rely on colour. It uses the explicit 认写 ("learn and write") lists in each 汉字 section instead. Those lists match the purple words wherever the colour is legible (L2: 吗 妈妈 不 呢 哥哥 丁).
- **L1–6 divorce characters from the text** (Preface p.VI). The texts are pinyin-led, and the characters are taught separately: "sixty common, easily learnt characters" plus components. **From L7 the text is in characters with pinyin beneath** (p.VII).
- **Lessons 1–6 are the "first cycle"**: "exposed to various basic sentence patterns … although grammar is not discussed systematically at this stage" (p.VI). Systematic grammar starts with L7.
- **L6 end-of-unit summary** (p.76): 21 initials, 38 finals, 4 tones; "more than one hundred words and expressions", "forty key sentences", "twenty-two sentences of classroom Chinese", "sixty basic Chinese characters" and "more than fifty new vocabulary items formed from them".
- **Characters** (p.2): Ding Libo 丁力波 (Canadian, M, 21; father Gubo, mother Ding Yun), Ma Dawei 马大为 (American, M, 22), Lin Na 林娜 (British, F, 19), Song Hua 宋华 (Chinese student, M, 20), Wang Xiaoyun 王小云 (Chinese student, F, 20), Lu Yuping 陆雨平 (reporter, M, 26), Chen laoshi 陈老师 (F, 30), Zhang jiaoshou 张教授 (M, 48), Yang laoshi 杨老师 (M, 32).
- **Pinyin-only drill words are NOT vocabulary.** Every lesson's 语音练习 lists words for pronunciation only, with an English gloss and no characters: yéye, mèimei, kělè, Fǎguó, Rìběn, jiějie, tàitai, Yīngyǔ, túshūguǎn, Xīnjiāpō… They are exposure for sound, not taught words. Listen-and-repeat (听述) lines are also printed in characters that are not always new words (e.g. L6 没听清楚). Treat both as "heard, not taught".
