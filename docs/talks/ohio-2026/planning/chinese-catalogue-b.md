**Page offset: printed page = PDF page − 24** (printed p.95 = PDF p.119). Front matter: the TOC (目录) runs across printed pp. 1–8 (PDF 7–14), and the preface, numbered in Roman numerals, starts at I (= PDF 15).

## Conventions that apply to all of Lessons 8–14 (book-level evidence)

| Piece | What the book does | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 语音 Phonetics | **Lessons 8–14 have NO phonetics section.** Phonetics and pronunciation drills appear only in Lessons 1–6. In Lessons 7–14 the texts are in characters with pinyin printed underneath. | Preface p. VII–VIII; TOC pp. 4–8, where no 语音 heading is listed for L8–14 |
| Section layout | 课文 Text (two parts, (一)/(二), each with its own 生词 list) → 补充生词 Supplementary words → 注释 Notes → 练习与运用 Drills (KEY SENTENCES box) → 阅读和复述 Reading → 语法 Grammar → 汉字 Characters → 文化知识 Culture | every lesson |
| 补充生词 Supplementary words | The preface says supplementary words are "learned according to the learners' individual abilities", i.e. optional. They show up in drills with pinyin printed next to them. | Preface p. VII |
| `*` before a new word | The book never explains it. The vocabulary index lists most starred words under two lesson numbers (e.g. 弟弟 2,8; 宿舍 5,13; 咖啡 2,13), so the star probably marks a word already met earlier and now taught in characters. Treat as **unclear**. | e.g. p. 97; index pp. 225–235 |
| Characters to WRITE vs only recognise | Every lesson has two headings: "认写基本汉字 Learn and write basic characters" and "认写课文中的汉字 Learn and write the characters appearing in the texts". **Both say learn AND write. The book never makes a recognise-only category.** Small grey items between the entries (components such as 灬, 犭, 乑) are presented as components with stroke orders. | e.g. pp. 106–109 |
| Reading passages | Words not yet taught appear inside the reading text, glossed in brackets (e.g. 年 nián, 前 qián, 经济 jīngjì, 常常 at p. 104). These words are *seen*, not *taught*. | pp. 104, 122, 141, 159, 178, 198, 216 |
| Totals | **The book prints no total number of words or characters anywhere** (checked: preface, TOC, both indexes). The vocabulary index (pp. 225–235, lesson numbers 1–14) lists main entries only. Morphemes listed underneath a word (e.g. 少 under 多少) mostly have no index entry of their own. Supplementary words have their own index (pp. 236–239). Character index pp. 240–242. **The TOC is wrong here: it says the Character Index starts at p. 236, but it actually starts at p. 240.** The character index also has visible lesson-number errors (虫 "19", 昨 "13", 题 "11"). | pp. 224–242 |

## Lesson 8 你们家有几口人 (pp. 95–110)

**Characters:** 林娜 Lin Na (British student), 王小云 Wang Xiaoyun (Chinese student), 贝贝 Beibei (Lin Na's dog).

**课文 Text:**
- (一) p. 95–96 [谈家庭 talking about family; 问职业 asking someone's job]:
  - asks whether the photo is of Xiaoyun's family (这是不是你们家的照片?);
  - asks how many people are in the family (你们家有几口人? 四口人);
  - lists the family members (爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和我);
  - Lin Na gives her own family (妈妈，一个姐姐和两个弟弟，一共六口人);
  - asks "who else?" (还有谁? 还有贝贝);
  - jokes about whether the dog counts as one of the family (小狗也是一口人吗?);
  - shows a photo (一张贝贝的照片) and says it is cute (真可爱);
  - asks whether Xiaoyun's family has a dog (没有);
  - asks whether Lin Na has a boyfriend (你有没有男朋友?);
  - asks his job (他做什么工作? 他是医生).
- (二) p. 98 [谈学校 talking about one's university]:
  - asks whether the institute is big (大不大? 不太大);
  - asks how many departments (多少个系? 十二个系);
  - asks whether Xiaoyun likes her department (很喜欢);
  - asks how many teachers (二十八个中国老师，十一个外国老师);
  - Lin Na's department: 有一百个，都是中国人，没有外国老师.

## Lesson 9 他今年二十岁 (pp. 111–129)

**Characters:**
- (一): Wang Xiaoyun and Lin Na.
- (二): Lin Na, 宋华 Song Hua (Chinese student, the birthday boy), 马大为 Ma Dawei, 丁力波 Ding Libo, Wang Xiaoyun.

**课文 Text:**
- (一) pp. 111–113 [约会 making an appointment; 问年龄和出生地 asking age and birthplace]:
  - greeting 你怎么样? 忙不忙? and asking about classes tomorrow morning (明天上午你有没有课?);
  - asks the day of the week (明天是星期几? 星期四); answer 上午、下午都有课;
  - asks about Sunday: free time? (你星期日有时间吗?), the date (星期日是几号? 十月二十七号), and that it is Song Hua's birthday;
  - asks his age (是吗? 他今年多大?);
  - his birth date and zodiac (一九八二年十月二十七日出生，属狗，今年二十岁);
  - asks where he is from (他是哪儿人? 北京人);
  - invites Lin Na to the party (聚会…你参加不参加? 太好了!我当然参加);
  - asks whether Chinese people eat cake on birthdays (中国人生日吃蛋糕吗?);
  - offers what to bring (我买一个大蛋糕，好吗? / 我买两瓶红葡萄酒).
- (二) pp. 114–115 [祝贺生日 celebrating a birthday]:
  - birthday wish (祝你生日快乐!) and thanks (谢谢…你们来，我很高兴);
  - talk about the food and drink: roast duck (我很喜欢吃烤鸭), wine (喝什么酒?), longevity noodles (还吃寿面; 真有意思);
  - asks Lin Na's birthday (你的生日是哪天? 十一月十二号) and plans to meet again that day (再来吃寿面).

## Lesson 10 我在这儿买光盘 (pp. 130–149)

**Characters:**
- (一): Wang Xiaoyun and Ma Dawei, in a shop.
- (二): 师傅 (a fruit seller) and Ding Libo.

**课文 Text:**
- (一) pp. 130–131 [喜欢不喜欢 likes and dislikes]:
  - asks what Dawei is buying (你在这儿买什么? 音乐光盘);
  - asks whether he comes here often (你常常来这儿吗? 不常来…星期天常常跟林娜去小商场);
  - asks what music he likes (你喜欢什么音乐? 中国音乐);
  - asks about one CD (这张光盘怎么样? 很好，是《梁祝》，很有名);
  - asks whether the shop has books and newspapers (这儿有没有书和报? 没有书，也没有报);
  - asks about notebooks (本子呢? 在那儿买。跟我来).
- (二) pp. 132–133 [解决语言困难 solving language problems; 买东西 shopping]:
  - the seller asks what he wants (先生，您要什么?);
  - Libo asks what something is (请问，这是什么? 香蕉苹果);
  - Libo explains he meant the Chinese word (对不起，我是问：这个汉语怎么说?);
  - the seller asks where he works (您在哪儿工作? 我在语言学院学习) and confirms he studies Chinese (您学习汉语，是不是?);
  - the seller teaches the names (这叫香蕉…那是葡萄);
  - Libo asks the price (一斤苹果多少钱? 三块二毛钱);
  - Libo complains it is expensive (真贵) and the seller bargains (三块二不贵…做个朋友，三块钱一斤);
  - asks the price of bananas (两块七毛五分一斤，五块钱两斤);
  - Libo orders (我买三斤香蕉和两斤香蕉苹果);
  - the seller gives the total (一共十四块钱), adds a free apple (再送您一个苹果) and asks if he wants anything else (您还要什么? 不要了，谢谢);
  - payment and change (您给我二十块钱，我找您六块钱), then goodbye.

| **了 (1)** | **Only the aspect 了 after the verb = realisation / completion.** 你买了几个苹果? vs 你买几个苹果?. ## Lesson 14 祝你圣诞快乐 [复习 Review] (pp. 206–223)

**Characters:** Ma Dawei, Ding Libo, and 丁云 Ding Yun (Libo's mother, on the phone). **Only one dialogue text in this lesson** (pp. 206–208), with one word list.

**课文 Text** (pp. 206–208):
- [抱怨与致歉 complaint / apology]:
  - Dawei passes on a message (上午十点半你妈妈给你打了一个电话。我告诉她你不在。我让她中午再给你打);
  - Libo says what he did (我刚才去邮局给我妈妈寄了点儿东西) and complains about the dirty clothes (我今天打扫了宿舍，你的脏衣服太多了);
  - Dawei apologises (不好意思。这两天我太忙了，我想星期六一起洗).
- [转达问候 passing on regards]:
  - Dawei answers the phone (喂，你好，你找谁? …请等一下);
  - mother and son exchange greetings and ask about health (你和爸爸身体怎么样? …你外婆身体好吗?);
  - Libo passes on regards (她让我问你们好。我们也问她好).
- Family and life news:
  - news of the brothers (哥哥在一个中学打工，弟弟在南方旅行; 我们都很想你们);
  - the mother asks about the dorm (你住的宿舍大不大? 住几个人?): two to a room, and his roommate (我跟一个美国人住);
  - Libo's Chinese friends help him study (帮助我念生词、复习课文、练习口语…问他们语法问题).
- [节日祝愿 holiday greetings]:
  - Christmas in China and gifts (今年你要在中国过圣诞节…送你一件圣诞礼物 / 我也给你们寄了圣诞礼物);
  - travel plans (欧洲旅行 / 我要去上海旅行);
  - holiday wishes (祝你旅行快乐! 我也祝你和爸爸圣诞快乐!).
