# L'Atelier A1 — what each teaching piece covers, Bienvenue + Units 1–4

Sources. "Bk p.N" means the printed page number of the student book (Manuel). The scan in the reordered PDF covers book pp. 6–11 (the table of contents), 12–15, 17–29, 31–43, 45–57 and 59–67. **Book pp. 16, 30, 44, 58 and 68–71 are NOT in the scan.** So Unit 4's Ateliers and Mémo come only from the teacher's guide. "G01", "G23" and "G4" are the three teacher's guides. "Test" means the guide's photocopiable unit test. Those tests sometimes go past the book's own boxes, and this file flags each place where they do.

The rule used below: a form is **in scope** if it appears in a grammar, vocabulary or Mémo box, or is practised in an activity or its answer key. A form that appears only once inside a document or transcript is marked *incidental*.

## C. Telling the time: Dan's statement checked

**Mostly confirmed, with one correction.** The book uses only the « (XX) heures (XX) » shape, on a 24-hour clock with spoken minutes. **Et quart, et demie and moins le quart never appear** in the box, the Mémo, the transcripts or the answer keys. The examples are *neuf heures quinze / trente / quarante-cinq*, *20 h 30*, *22 h 45*, *19 h 45* and *18 h 30*.

**BUT *midi* and *minuit* ARE in scope.** The Unit 2 "L'heure" box (Bk p.36) and the Mémo "Indiquer l'heure" (Bk p.43) both end with « Il est midi. Il est minuit. » Activity 4 then asks for them: « Il est …. (12h) » / « Il est …. (24h) ». Its answer key (G23 Piste 49) reads « – Excusez-moi, vous avez l'heure ? – Il est midi. » and « Quelle heure il est ? – Il est minuit. » *Midi* is also a moment of the day as early as Bienvenue (*dimanche midi*, Bk p.14).

Page record for midi/minuit: **Bk p.36** (L'heure box, plus activity 4 rows a and b), **Bk p.43** (Mémo « Indiquer l'heure »), **G23 Pistes 47 and 49** (transcripts), and **Bk p.14** (*dimanche midi* as a moment). The searches for « et quart », « demie » and « moins le » across all three guides returned 0 hits, and none of these appears on any scanned book page. They remain out.

Not covered: *une heure* (singular) is never shown; *du matin / de l'après-midi / du soir* after a time; *pile*; *environ*.
